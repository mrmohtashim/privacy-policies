ShotForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Overview

ShotForge ("the app") is made by Apptrica. ShotForge helps you design App Store and Google Play screenshots on your device. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. ShotForge does not require an account and does not run its own servers.

Your designs and images

Projects, layers, text and settings are stored only on your device. Images you choose from your photo library are copied into the app's private storage so they can appear in your designs. Exported screenshots are saved to your photo library only when you tap Export. None of this content is uploaded to us or shared with anyone. Deleting a project, or uninstalling the app, removes it from the app.

Photo library access

The app asks for permission to add exported screenshots to your photo library, and lets you pick images to place inside device mockups. You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

Advertising

ShotForge is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier (only where you allow it), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device and app information, ad interactions, and crash and performance diagnostics. This information is collected by Google, not by Apptrica, and is used under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. More about how Google uses data from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

If you are in a region that requires it (for example the EEA, the UK, Switzerland or some US states), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy choices. On iOS, the app only accesses the advertising identifier if you allow tracking; you can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant.

Usage limits

The app stores a small counter on your device to apply the free daily export allowance and remember which templates you unlocked. It is not linked to your identity and is not sent to us.

Children

ShotForge is a design tool for app developers and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and retention

Because your content stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security. We do not hold any copy of it. Data processed by Google for advertising is retained according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The latest version is always available in the app and on our website, with the date it was last updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Contact Apptrica using the support email shown on the app's App Store or Google Play page.
